# nfnh_identification (old notebook: NFNH.ipynb)
Script: `scripts/morefigures/food_prices_gap.py`. Tables only.
- `nfnh_postcheck_v002.csv/.tex`: how the v0.02 benchmark sets NFNH (non-food, non-housing) costs. First, the tool's CPI share times the food cost (national CPI weights). This is then post-checked against the 15 participant households' reported spending on education, transport and health. The table shows the tool estimate, the participants' average per person, the deviation factor and the final NFNH. In Mukono the factor is 0, giving NFNH of 664,544 UGX/month. In Nakaseke, education spending is about 10x the tool share, so the factor is +8.3%, raising NFNH from 599,526 to 649,487 UGX/month. The table also gives the median per participant household.
- `nfnh_survey_inputs.csv`: which survey answers feed the education, health and transport post-check, and the rule used for each (from `scripts/benchmark/build_participants.py`).

**What changed:** the old notebook keyword-searched a column dictionary for health, transport, clothing and education words and listed 39 matching columns. Several did not hold spending, for example education levels of household members. That list is replaced by the variables and rules the rebuilt benchmark actually uses. Values come from the v0.02 rebuild, which is recalculated in a temporary folder and checked against the published NFNH (see costing/NOTES.md). Clothing is not asked as spending in the survey, so it enters only through the CPI share.
